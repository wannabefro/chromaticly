Where they actually differ

Seed qualityOne item per skill — a coin-flip either way. Realistically a right/wrong split: grade 3 or grade 1.Brackets each skill to within a grade. This is a genuine estimate.
Drop-off riskLow. Two minutes is inside anyone’s patience.Real. Seven steps shown up front, before any value has been delivered.
Teaches the modelImplicitly — the strand overline changes each item.Explicitly — the board IS the seven-lane model, met before 7a.
ResumableNo — 8 items or nothing.Yes — per skill, and untested skills read honestly as untested.
Cost of being wrongThe same, and low. Nothing is locked (R2), depths move with every answer, and 7c already tells the learner they drift. A bad seed self-corrects within a session or two.
Build costItem bank + a fixed script.Item bank + a per-strand ladder with stop rules, and a resumable partial-placement state.
Recommendation — A now, B as a per-skill re-test
Ship A. Under spine C the seed is cheap to be wrong about — that is the whole point of the model — so paying seven minutes up front buys precision the system does not need, at the one moment where the learner has the least patience and the least reason to trust us.
But keep B’s ladder and attach it where it is worth its cost: 7c currently says “tap a skill to nudge it up or down”, which is self-assessment. Make that tap open B’s ladder for that one skill instead — four questions, that skill only. The learner who disagrees with their seed gets a real answer rather than a slider, and B’s machinery earns its build cost by also serving every later “have I actually moved?” moment, not just onboarding.
If you’d rather ship B
It is the better product if the audience arrives motivated — an adult revising for a specific grade will happily spend seven minutes to be placed properly, and the board teaches the model for free. The case against is entirely about first-run patience. Two things would change the call: evidence that the audience is goal-driven rather than casual, or making B’s first skill deliver a lesson before asking for the other six.
