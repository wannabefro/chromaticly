Counting in 6/8
In compound time the beat divides into three. Six quavers group into two dotted-crotchet beats — count 1 2 3 · 4 5 6.
💡
SMART TIP
Feel 6/8 as a gentle two, not a fast six — like the swing of “merri‑ly, merri‑ly”.
