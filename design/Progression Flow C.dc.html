Two kinds, and only one is new. Within-lane prerequisites are pervasive and already free — chord-inversions-5 tells you to find the triad first, which is just chords at grade 4, i.e. the lane’s own depth order. Cross-lane prerequisites are real but sparse and cluster at grade 5: transposing-instruments-5 is filed under pitch yet needs major 2nds and perfect 5ths (intervals) and the transposed key signature (scales).
The graph must be authored, not derived. Today’s unlocks chain is a serpentine tour, not a dependency: all 20 of its cross-strand edges stay inside one grade and none cross a grade boundary. Converting it would import false dependencies like chords-4 → ornaments-4. Estimated real cross-lane edges: 8–12, grades 3–5.

Practice stays top level. Under C it is the only surface that is not lane-scoped, so nesting it inside the lane-structured tab would be the wrong shape — and the shell exists precisely because Practice was once unreachable. Each tab keeps a distinct job: Learn is lane-scoped forward motion, Practice is cross-lane retention, Exams is the gate, Profile is identity and settings.
What DOES merge — the radar
The lanes and the profile’s 7-strand radar are one object seen twice: lanes are the radar, unrolled. Learn owns the interactive version you enter; Profile keeps a compact read-only summary next to identity and settings. They must never disagree — today the radar counts locked lessons, so an unreached strand reads empty; under C that rollup has to change or the two views will tell different stories.
